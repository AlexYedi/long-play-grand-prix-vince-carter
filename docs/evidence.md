# Evidence: problems, value and opportunities, with sources

One table per question: what's broken in LP reporting today, who says so, what our product changes, and which organizer challenge clause it answers (IDs from `docs/synthesis.md`). Compiled 2026-10-24 from `research/` plus a fresh source pass.

**Source tiers.** Use the higher tiers on stage.
- **Primary:** regulator, public LP disclosure, SEC filing, standards body, peer-reviewed or foundation study
- **Survey:** a named survey; sample size noted when it's disclosed
- **Secondary:** trade press or analyst reporting someone else's data
- **Vendor:** a company selling into this market, quoting its own data
- **⚠ Unverified:** we couldn't open the source; don't say it out loud

Challenge IDs: **F1** "LPs commit capital for ten years and get PDFs in return." · **F2** "Quarterly reports arrive in different formats." · **F3** "Valuation marks move with little explanation." · **F4** "Judging a GP's real track record means rebuilding it by hand." · **P1** "40 updates in 40 formats, when it gets them at all." · **P2** "Partners learn a company is in trouble when it asks for a bridge." · **E1** "Spreadsheets, gut feel and endless inbox triage."

## 3. Gaps, and what not to say

- **GP hours per quarter: no independent figure exists.** Vendors claim "38 hours per month" manual vs. 3.5 automated ([Standard Metrics](https://www.standardmetrics.io)) and "3–4 weeks per quarter" (Visible). Present these only as "vendors claim," and get the real number from GPs in the room. The pricing math in `research/market-map.md` depends on it (45% confidence).
- **No public Fund I/II LP letter with numbers** turned up in the search. Use that as proof of the confidentiality point, not as a hole in the research.
- **⚠ Unverified, don't quote:** CalPERS's "2-quarter delay" line (the page wouldn't load; check it in a browser first) · Carta's DPI figures (37% of 2019 vintages had made any distribution; the page returned 403) · Oregon's "IRRs have not been approved by the GPs" footnote (seen only in a search snippet) · "~40% of venture funds have K-1 delays or errors" (no named source).
- **Dropped, unusable:** "73%" and "92%" LP-dissatisfaction figures (vendor blogs) · "78% of LPs would withdraw over poor reporting" (no underlying survey) · Preqin's "69% want more detailed reporting" (only second-hand).

## The three lines for a 3-minute pitch

1. **Problem, in the LP's own words:** WSIB publishes GP-reported IRRs that "have not been confirmed by the general partners."
2. **Why it matters to an emerging manager:** Kauffman found venture funds report peak IRRs right before "serial fundraising in month twenty-seven," so LPs have learned to discount early marks. Provable marks are how a Fund I earns its Fund II.
3. **Proof that ours works:** extraction accuracy next to the do-nothing baseline from `data-pack/eval/score.py` (carry-forward: 18.2% field accuracy, 12.5% flag recall, 0% traceback).
