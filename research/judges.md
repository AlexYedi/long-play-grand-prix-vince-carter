# Judges: who's in the room and what the demo shows them

**Event:** Deal Flow, NYC's first VC hackathon. Hosted by localhost:nyc and VCLab at betaworks on 2026-10-24: one day, teams of four, live demos judged by NYC fund partners and operators. The prize is intros to the judges' funds. No scoring rubric was published, so the working rule is from the event page: "both slick code and a real business."

**Our track:** Track 4, Funds: "make LPs as informed as their GPs." Built GP-side, for emerging managers.

Everything below comes from public sources: the event page, fund and company sites, and the judges' own posts and essays.

## What the panel points to

No judge has written about LP reporting. Four point the same way: **trust comes from inspection and approval, and value comes from owning a workflow with a measurable outcome, not from model IQ.** Lead with the approval gate, not the model.

## What the demo must show

1. **Every number traces to its source:** click any figure in the letter and land on the founder's own sentence (Radhika, D.J.).
2. **Reconciliation against last quarter:** the delta table, with unexplained marks flagged before they reach the letter (Garrett, Michael).
3. **Accuracy next to the do-nothing baseline:** the scorer against last quarter's values carried forward, which gets 18.2% field accuracy, 12.5% flag recall and 0% traceback (Radhika, Garrett, Michael).
4. **The data perimeter:** redaction before any model call, and GP approval before an LP sees anything (Bettina, Abeera).
5. **No new format for founders:** messy emails, decks and exports in, as they arrive today (D.J., Michael).
6. **The business:** hours today vs. with the tool, cost per letter, and who pays (Garrett, Michael).
